THE RESEARCH GAP 01

Learning together
shouldn’t mean
sharing everything.

Federated learning keeps medical images at each institution while allowing a shared model to improve. But model updates can still carry clues about the data used to train them.

MedFedAI makes that risk the focus: define what an attacker can see, test for information leakage, then evaluate a measurable privacy protection.

THE PROJECT IN FOCUS 04

Three questions.
One careful study.

A practical investigation into privacy-aware federated medical image classification.

01

Can updates reveal private information?

Define the server’s view and test whether shared client updates enable a controlled inference or reconstruction attack.

02

What changes with differential privacy?

Apply update clipping and calibrated noise, recording the privacy parameters and budget for each experiment.

03

What are the trade-offs?

Compare privacy test outcomes and classification performance, then report limits from compute, communication, and the chosen setup.
