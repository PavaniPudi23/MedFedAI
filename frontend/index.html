PRIVACY IN THIS PROTOTYPE

Settings are illustrative here.

The dashboard simulates round progress and shows clipping and noise controls. It does not train on images, apply noise to real gradients, or calculate an epsilon privacy guarantee.

For the actual PyTorch training prototype, use train_federated.py. It clips each client contribution, applies Gaussian noise, and writes an Opacus RDP accountant estimate with its assumptions in the privacy report.
